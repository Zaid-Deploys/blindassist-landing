An offline voice for what's in front of you.

BlindAssist narrates the world in real time for visually impaired users — obstacles, people, text, and Indian currency — entirely on-device. No internet connection required, ever.

Obstacle & hazard detection

Depth estimation flags what's ahead and roughly how far — chairs, steps, curbs — before you reach them.

continuous

People & object recognition

Narrates who and what is nearby as the scene changes, without needing to ask.

volume up

Read text aloud

Point the camera at a sign, label, or page — press once, hear it read back on demand.

volume down

Indian currency ID

Identifies note and coin denominations, trained specifically on Indian currency.
